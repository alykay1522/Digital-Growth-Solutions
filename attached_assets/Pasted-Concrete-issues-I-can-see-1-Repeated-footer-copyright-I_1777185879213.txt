Concrete issues I can see
1. Repeated footer copyright
Issue:  
The footer text is duplicated three times:

© 2026 Digital Growth Solutions Agency. All rights reserved.
© 2026 Digital Growth Solutions Agency. All rights reserved.
© 2026 Digital Growth Solutions Agency. All rights reserved.

How to fix it:

In your layout/footer component, you’re almost certainly rendering the copyright block multiple times (e.g., inside a loop or nested component).

Make sure it’s only rendered once, e.g.:

jsx
<footer className="site-footer">
  <p>© 2026 Digital Growth Solutions Agency. All rights reserved.</p>
  <a href="/privacy-policy">Privacy Policy</a>
  <a href="/terms-of-service">Terms of Service</a>
</footer>
Check that no parent component also injects the same text.

2. Brand/domain mismatch (potential trust friction)
Issue:

Domain: digitalgrowthsolutions.org

Brand/email: Digital Growth Solutions Agency / hello@digitalgrowthsolutionsagency.com

This isn’t “broken,” but it can create a subtle trust wobble for small‑biz owners.

How to fix it:

Either:

Add a short line in the footer or hero like:
“Digital Growth Solutions Agency operates at digitalgrowthsolutions.org and digitalgrowthsolutionsagency.com.”

Or align domains over time (e.g., redirect .org → .com or vice versa).

3. Capacity line that may not be enforced
Issue:  
Hero line:

“Sites from $495 · Accepting 3 new clients”

If this isn’t actually enforced by logic (e.g., you don’t auto‑hide it when full), it can become untrue and look gimmicky.

How to fix it:

Option A: Make it evergreen:

“Sites from $495 · Limited spots available each month”

Option B: Wire it to a config flag:

js
const acceptingClients = true;
const capacityText = acceptingClients
  ? "Accepting 3 new clients"
  : "Join the waitlist for next month";
Then render capacityText in the hero.

4. Service/section links likely need URL checks
From the content I see:

“View All Services”

“Explore All 8 AI Agents”

“Learn how it works”

“Pay Online”

“Blog”

“Contact”

“Privacy Policy”

“Terms of Service”

What to verify manually:

Each of those is actually clickable and goes to a real, relevant page.

None of them 404 or point to placeholder content.

How to fix broken ones:

For each missing page, either:

Create a simple stub page with correct route, or

Temporarily remove the link until the page exists.

Example (React Router):

jsx
<Link to="/services">View All Services</Link>
Make sure /services is defined in your router.
